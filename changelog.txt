Version: 0.10.0
  Features:
    - The Space Platform Access Port (SPAP) is a large space-platform-only structure that connects to the space platform hub's inventory through the construction infrastructure of the space platform foundation, and allows inserter access distant from the platform hub.
  Graphics:
    - Changed the appearance of big chest extenders to use a different sprite.
  Balancing:
    - Linking distance of chest extenders can now be modified in startup settings.
      - Bonus distance from quality, also.
    - Chest extenders can now connect semi-diagonally, one tile off from orthogonal.
    - SPAPs are limited to 2 per platform.
      - Quality space platform hubs and quality SPAPs increase this number by 4 per quality level.
      - These numbers can also be changed in settings.
  Modding:
    - Added best-effort compatibility with mods that alter chest surface conditions (but forget ProxyContainers) in data-updates stage
